## Pillar 1: Logging - The Agent's Diary

What are Logs? Logs are the atomic unit of observability. Think of them as timestamped entries in your agent's diary. Each entry is a raw, immutable fact about a discrete event: "At 10:01:32, I was asked a question. At 10:01:33, I decided to use the get\_weather tool." They tell us what happened.
